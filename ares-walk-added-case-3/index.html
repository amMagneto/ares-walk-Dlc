      MISSION PROFILE 4-Hour Dual-Astronaut Surface EVA

      Pressurized Rover Base Anchor

      01

      WHY THIS SITE MATTERS

      Web-scale Mars atlases show regional geology, but boots-on-the-ground explorers need hyperlocalized trafficability and life-support physics. In the Altadena boxwork unit, intersecting networks of mineralized bedrock ridges (1.2–1.8 m high, ~5 m wide) surround sunken polygonal hollows filled with loose, high-slip aeolian sand.

      02

      HOW ARES-WALK SOLVES THE TRAVERSE

      A

      Multi-Sensor Fusion

      Co-registers 1 m/px HiRISE stereo topography, THEMIS nighttime thermal inertia, and CRISM hyperspectral mineralogy onto a unified local Martian grid.

      B

      Suit-Calibrated Routing

      Replaces shortest-distance paths with an asymmetric 0.38g suited metabolic cost surface—preferring firm bedrock ridge crests over energy-draining sand traps.

      C

      Hard Flight-Rule Safety

      Enforces a mandatory 25% PLSS walkback reserve at every single metre of the traverse, maps UHF line-of-sight dead zones, and supports instant Solar Particle Event (SPE) minimum-time abort re-routing.
